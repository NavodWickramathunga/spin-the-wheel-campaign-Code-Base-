Usage report (CSV)
Vouchers don't all have to be given out. Unused vouchers stay in the shared pool, and the next campaign uses them automatically - there's nothing to move. “Removed” vouchers were taken off by a Replace current prizes upload; they were never given out and come back if you upload them again. Expired vouchers can't be used.

By reward

Used % = given out ÷ uploaded. “Left for next campaign” = unused + removed, not expired.
